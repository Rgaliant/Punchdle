How to play

Every day there's a secret sweet spot: a hold time somewhere between 1.5 and 6.5 seconds. It's the same for everyone.

  1. Press and hold the button (or spacebar) to wind up.
  2. Let go to throw the punch.
  3. The closer your release is to the sweet spot, the more punch points you get, up to 999.

You get a rough hint, but no timer. Go by feel. One punch per day. Refreshing mid-hold counts as a whiff.
